# Aether

> A digital medium for creating, performing, and experiencing living audiovisual worlds.

**Aether** is an experimental WebGPU project exploring what happens when real-time graphics, sound, interaction, and generative systems become one medium.

It is not intended to be another music visualizer or another node-based creative editor.

The goal is to create **living digital matter** — systems that flow, react, evolve, collapse, and transform in response to sound and interaction.

---

## The idea

Most music visualizers work like this:

```text
audio → parameters → animation
```

Aether aims for something closer to:

```text
audio → forces → matter → behavior → emergence
```

Bass might become pressure.

A kick might create an impulse.

Mid frequencies might generate turbulence.

Energy might change viscosity.

A beat might cause an entire structure to collapse.

The visual isn't simply *reacting* to the music.

**The music becomes part of the physics.**

---

## What Aether is

Aether sits somewhere between:

* a generative art environment
* a digital sculpture studio
* a VJ instrument
* a real-time simulation
* an audiovisual installation
* a collaborative creative space

The underlying systems are computational, but the experience should feel artistic rather than technical.

The goal is:

> **Beautiful first. Technical second.**

---

## The three experiences

### Gallery

The first thing you see should be the artwork.

Not an editor.

Not a parameter panel.

A collection of evolving digital worlds that can be explored and entered.

Scenes are not simply static presets. They are generative systems with their own:

* matter
* fields
* forces
* materials
* lighting
* audio relationships
* environments
* rules

A scene should feel alive even when nobody is touching it.

---

### Studio

The Studio is where worlds are created and shaped.

Instead of exposing every technical implementation detail, the interface is organized around artistic concepts.

#### Matter

* Fluid
* Dust
* Smoke
* Fragments
* Particles
* Geometry

#### Behavior

* Flow
* Attract
* Repel
* Grow
* Collapse
* Drift
* Pulse

#### Atmosphere

* Fog
* Light
* Depth
* Distortion
* Grain
* Bloom

The Studio should feel more like a **digital sculpture studio** than an IDE.

---

### Live

Press **Perform**.

The interface disappears.

The artwork becomes the interface.

Audio, mouse, keyboard, MIDI, cameras, and eventually external controllers can influence the system in real time.

The performer doesn't trigger predefined animations.

**They play the system.**

---

## A scene is a world

A scene isn't just a collection of effects.

It is a small generative universe.

```text
World
 ├── Matter
 ├── Fields
 ├── Forces
 ├── Materials
 ├── Light
 ├── Camera
 ├── Audio
 └── Post Processing
```

These systems continuously interact.

The same scene can produce different states depending on:

* sound
* time
* interaction
* random seed
* performer input
* previous state

The objective is not to make an animation.

It is to create a **system capable of generating an artwork**.

---

## Visual direction

Aether is inspired by generative art, experimental 3D, VJ culture, computational design, audiovisual installations, and digital sculpture.

The visual language is centered around:

* particles and fields
* fluid simulation
* procedural geometry
* volumetric light
* translucent materials
* data-driven forms
* organic motion
* strange physical systems
* slowly evolving compositions

Rather than:

* equalizers
* waveform visualizers
* generic neon effects
* preset-driven animations
* endless particle explosions

The intention is to create visuals that could exist as artworks independently of the software that created them.

---

## WebGPU

WebGPU is the foundation for the real-time simulation layer.

It makes it possible to experiment with large-scale GPU-driven systems directly in the browser:

* particle simulations
* fluid dynamics
* GPU physics
* procedural geometry
* volumetric effects
* feedback systems
* audio-reactive simulation
* generative environments

The browser becomes the canvas.

The GPU becomes the medium.

---

## Collaboration

Aether is ultimately intended to be collaborative.

A world can become a shared space where multiple people experience the same evolving simulation.

Eventually, participants may be able to influence the artwork without directly controlling it.

```text
multiple people
       ↓
individual inputs
       ↓
shared simulation
       ↓
one evolving artwork
```

The audience itself becomes another force inside the system.

## Current status

🚧 **Early experimental development**

The project is currently focused on establishing the core rendering and simulation architecture.

The initial milestone is deliberately small:

* [ ] WebGPU rendering foundation
* [ ] GPU particle system
* [ ] Procedural visual system
* [ ] Audio analysis
* [ ] Audio → physical forces
* [ ] Direct manipulation
* [ ] Minimal Studio
* [ ] Fullscreen Performance mode
* [ ] Scene saving
* [ ] Shareable worlds

The first shippable version will focus on **one exceptionally good generative world**, rather than a large collection of mediocre effects.

---

## The first milestone

The first version should be able to do this:

```text
Open Aether
    ↓
Enter a world
    ↓
Play music
    ↓
Shape the simulation
    ↓
Watch sound become physical behavior
    ↓
Press Perform
    ↓
Fullscreen artwork
    ↓
Share the world
```

If the interface disappears and the experience stops being interesting, the project has become an editor.

If the interface disappears and the experience becomes **more beautiful**, we're building the right thing.

## Long-term vision

The long-term goal is not a collection of visual effects.

It is an **instrument for audiovisual art**.

A place where people can create systems rather than animations, perform them rather than play presets, and experience them as living digital artworks.

Not the editor.

Not the parameters.

Not the technology.

**The world.**
